<h1>AROMIQO INDIA – Brand Introduction</h1>
AROMIQO INDIA is a dehydrated food ingredients business specializing in Garlic Powder, Onion Powder, and Ginger Powder.
Our products provide convenient flavour and aroma for everyday cooking, making food preparation easier and more consistent.
<br>
<h3>🧄 Garlic Powder</h3>
Garlic Powder is a convenient way to add garlic flavour to:
Curries & gravies
Sauces & dips
Seasonings
Snacks
Garlic bread & savoury preparations
<br>
<h3>🧅 Onion Powder</h3>
Onion Powder provides convenient onion flavour without the need for peeling and chopping fresh onions.
Ideal for:
Gravies & sauces
Seasoning blends
Snacks
Soups
Ready-to-cook preparations
<h3>🫚 Ginger Powder</h3>
Ginger Powder offers an easy way to incorporate ginger into everyday food preparation.
Tea & beverages
Curries
Marinades
Spice blends
Various Indian recipes
